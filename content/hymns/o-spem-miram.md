O spem miram, quam dedisti
mortis hora te flentibus,
dum post mortem promisisti
te profuturum fratribus.
Imple, Pater, quod dixisti,
nos tuis iuvans precibus.

Qui tot signis claruisti
in aegrorum corporibus,
nobis opem ferens Christi,
aegris medere moribus.
Imple, Pater, quod dixisti,
nos tuis iuvans precibus.

## English

O wondrous hope, which you gave
to those who wept for you at the hour of your death,
when you promised, after your passing,
that you would help your brethren still.
Fulfill, O Father, what you have spoken,
and help us by your prayers.

You who shone with so many wonders
worked upon the bodies of the sick,
bring to us the help of Christ,
and heal the sickness of our ways.
Fulfill, O Father, what you have spoken,
and help us by your prayers.
